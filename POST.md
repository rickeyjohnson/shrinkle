**Thread title:** Shrinkle

**Theme:** Your answers literally shrink. Each word sets the next letter box at one letter shorter than what you typed, and your points pull you down through 12 layers of scale, from the Cosmic Web to an atomic nucleus.

**Controls:**
- Click **Start shrinking** (or press Enter).
- Type your answer (spaces don't count), press **Enter** to submit, and **Backspace** to delete. On a phone, tap the box to type.
- No skipping: if the 20 seconds run out, you score 0 and stay on your layer. One-letter typos are forgiven on 4+ letter answers.
- 7 rounds × 20 seconds, then the "Dive complete" screen (under 3 minutes).

**How it plays:** Each round gives you a category (5,200 possible, like "Vegetables", "Fast food chains", "Animals starting with L"). Points = length + time (10 per letter + 3 per second left, about 1,000 max), and every point threshold is a deeper layer, so a big round can skip layers. Long answers score more but short ones box you in. Everyone gets the same daily dive, so copy your result and compare.
