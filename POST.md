**Thread title:** Shrinkle

**Theme:** Every word you type must be shorter than your last one, so you're forced to shrink your answers round by round while the camera zooms from the Milky Way down to an atom.

**Controls:**
- Click **Play today's puzzle** (or press Enter) to start.
- Type the word (spaces don't count), press **Enter** to submit, and **Backspace** to delete.
- **Esc** or the **Skip round** button skips a category (0 points).
- 7 rounds × 20 seconds. You always reach the results screen in under 3 minutes.

**How it plays:** Each round has a new category (5,200 possible, like "African countries", "Pokémon ending in -ON", "Sports"). Score = letters × (10 + seconds left), and obscure "deep cut" answers get ×1.5. Everyone gets the same daily puzzle, so paste your emoji result and compare.
